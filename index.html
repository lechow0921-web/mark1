说的一方以为对方会听到什么，对方真正听到、真正在意的又是什么。每个案例都写明了证据，也写明了局限。What the sender believed the audience would hear, and what the audience actually heard and cared about. Each case states its evidence and its limits.

叙事NarrativeUCL 硕士论文 · 国家品牌UCL dissertation · nation branding2025 — 2026

说给世界听的故事，和边缘群体经历的现实The story told to the world, and the reality a marginalised group lives

以为Assumed

对外讲好一个包容的国家故事，就能赢得全球受众的信任。Tell the world a good story of inclusion and global audiences will trust it.

发现Found

当对外叙事跑在对内实质前面，落差本身就成了公信力风险：边缘群体关于生计与参与决策的诉求缺席，而海外媒体和受众迟早会看到这道缝隙。When the outward story runs ahead of domestic substance, the gap itself becomes a credibility risk: the group's claims on livelihood and a say in decisions stay absent, and foreign media and audiences eventually see the seam.

这对品牌传播意味着什么Why it matters for brands

品牌也一样：讲给受众听的故事必须有实质撑着。叙事跑在实质前面，短期能提升形象，长期会透支信任。好的传播，是先弄清楚受众真正会怎么听，再决定怎么讲。研究基于政策文本与公开资料。Brands work the same way: the story has to be backed by substance. A narrative that outruns reality lifts the image short term and spends trust long term. Good communication starts from how the audience will actually hear it. 局限与验证设计Limits & test design

一条爆款推不出规律，偶然因素要和内容因素分开。验证方法：控制内容形式不变，分别发布小众选题和热门选题两组样本，再比较收藏率与分享率。One hit is not a rule; luck has to be separated from content. The test: hold format constant, publish a niche-topic set and a popular-topic set, then compare save and share rates.

§ 4方法Method

一个循环，三种证据One loop, three kinds of evidence

我做传播的方法来自研究训练。公共政策和品牌传播的内核相通：都要让一个具体的群体理解并接受一件事。所以先定义问题和受众，再去接触真实的人，用数据验证，最后修正表达。My communications method comes from research training. Public policy and brand communications share a core: getting a specific group to understand and accept something. So: define the problem and the audience, meet real people, test with data, then refine the message.

硕士论文 · UCLMSc dissertation · UCL

国家品牌与对外叙事：当实质跟不上传播Nation branding and outward narrative: when substance lags the story

以国家品牌（Nation Branding）为理论框架，用话语分析研究政府如何面向全球受众建构对外叙事；以一个边缘群体的处境作为对内实质，分析两者之间的话语差异，以及这道落差带来的公信力风险。Using nation-branding theory and discourse analysis, it examines how a government builds its story for global audiences, takes a marginalised group's situation as the domestic substance, and analyses the discursive gap between the two and the credibility risk it creates.

叙事可以先行，但不能脱离实质；被讲述的人，也应该是被倾听的人。A story can lead, but it can't leave the substance behind; the people being talked about should also be heard.
